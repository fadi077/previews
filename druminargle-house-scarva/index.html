Georgian country house · Scarva

A Georgian country house stay, built in 1720.

Druminargle House is a lovingly restored classical Georgian country house on Poyntzpass Road, Scarva, surrounded by mature gardens in a beautiful rural area, yet convenient to Newry and a good stopping point between Dublin and Belfast. Your host is Helen.

Why stay

History, gardens and a warm welcome

A house with a story

Built in 1720, and once the home of Charles Davis Lucas VC, the first recipient of the Victoria Cross.

Easy to reach

A good stop between Dublin and Belfast, and convenient to Newry.

Period rooms, modern comforts

Original features with en-suite bathrooms, Wi-Fi, TV and parking.
