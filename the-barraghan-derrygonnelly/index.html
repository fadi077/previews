Hideaway hut · Derrygonnelly, Co. Fermanagh

A quiet hut on the farm, with its own hot tub.

The Barraghan is a rustic hideaway on the edge of a farm at 78 Lisdead Road, Derrygonnelly, near Enniskillen. It has a private wood-fired hot tub, an en-suite shower room and a small kitchen, and sleeps two adults and one child under 10.

What to expect

Taken from the official Fermanagh Lakelands and Discover Northern Ireland listings.

A hut on the farm

The hideaway is at 78 Lisdead Road, Derrygonnelly, on the edge of the farm, with its own parking.

Sleeps two adults and one child

A double bed, plus a sofa bed suitable for one extra guest or a child under 10. No pets.

A private wood-fired hot tub

The tub is for your party only, outdoors under a pergola, and is subject to the weather. Children must be supervised.

A self-contained stay

En-suite shower, a small kitchen, an electric stove, linen, towels and two dressing gowns. Check-in from 15:00, check-out at 11:00. A car is needed.

Why stay

A slower stay in the Lakelands

Just for your group

A single hut, not a row of lodges, with a hot tub that only you use.

Derrygonnelly and Boho nearby

The village has shops, a coffee shop and pubs. Boho, a few miles away, has the Linnet Inn, the last thatched pub in the county, and was used for Game of Thrones filming.

Set up for comfort

En-suite shower, a kitchen, heating, Wi‑Fi and a TV, so you can stay in as easily as you can go out.

The Lakelands on the doorstep

Walks, caves, Lough Erne and the Lough Erne Resort golf course are all in easy reach.
