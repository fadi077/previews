Guest accommodation · 99 Main Street, Dungiven

A comfortable base under Benbradagh.

The Manor offers five private en-suite rooms in the centre of Dungiven, with a shared, fully equipped kitchen and a free continental breakfast. It's an easy touring base for Derry, Limavady, the Sperrins and the Causeway Coast.
